# Ottawa benchmark outputs

These files are generated by `scripts/upstream-loss-normalized.R`.

- `ottawa_processed_long.csv`: standardized measurements plus derived
  normalization targets and the combined `TOTAL` fraction.
- `ottawa_paired_ratios.csv`: positive, paired WWTP/site-D measurements and
  their log10 WWTP-to-neighbourhood ratios.
- `ottawa_apparent_loss_summary.csv`: sample size, quartiles of the log10 and
  linear ratios, and apparent-loss quartiles by biomarker, normalization, and
  sample fraction.
- `figures/figure_Ottawa_loss.png` and `.pdf`: revised main-text benchmark
  figure.
- `figures/figure_Ottawa_raw.png`: supplementary measurement-context figure.

Apparent loss is calculated as `(1 - WWTP/neighbourhood) * 100`. Negative
values mean that the WWTP measurement exceeded the neighbourhood measurement;
they do not indicate viral generation and can reflect downstream inputs,
mixing, dilution, solids transport, or analytical variability.

For N2 in the total fraction, the four median apparent-loss estimates are
34.59% (raw), 27.55% (PMMoV-normalized), 25.00% (TS-normalized), and 4.15%
(per-capita-flow adjusted), reproducing the manuscript's approximately 4–35%
range.
